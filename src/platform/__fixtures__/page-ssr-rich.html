AI search engines now answer a growing share of queries directly, and they decide what to cite from the structure and clarity of the pages they crawl. Generative engine optimization is the discipline of making your content the obvious answer rather than a supporting mention.

What is generative engine optimization?

Generative engine optimization, or GEO, is the practice of improving how often AI search engines cite your content in their generated answers.

How to improve AI visibility?

Publish clear question-based headings, direct answers, structured data, and visible publication dates on every page you want cited.

Why does structured data matter for AI?

Structured data gives AI crawlers explicit entities and relationships, which increases the confidence they have in citing your page.

Which platforms support llms.txt?

llms.txt is supported by an increasing number of AI platforms, and the proposal keeps gaining adoption across the ecosystem.

Can small sites compete in AI search?

Yes - AI search rewards clear, direct, well-structured answers over domain age alone, so smaller sites can win citations.
