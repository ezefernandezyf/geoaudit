What is the audit process?

The audit process is a structured evaluation of a website across five weighted dimensions. It runs entirely in the cloud and produces a single visibility score.

Each dimension follows a documented rubric that mirrors how AI assistants select sources. The results are deterministic for the same inputs.

A complete report is generated in under a minute. In 2025 the platform processed audits for hundreds of sites.

How is the methodology built?

The methodology is built from five weighted dimensions and validated against real audit results. Each dimension isolates a different part of the citation decision.

The weights follow published research on how assistants select passages. The 67% share of answer quality dominates the final number.

Calibration happens on a labeled set of pages. The whole pipeline stays fully deterministic.
